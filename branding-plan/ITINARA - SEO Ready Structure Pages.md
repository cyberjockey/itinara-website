# **ITINARA — SEO-Ready City Page Structure**

Struktur ini dirancang untuk **international SEO**, konversi penjualan itinerary, dan pengalaman pengguna yang jelas. Cocok untuk website & app.

---

## **1\. Hero Section (Above the Fold)**

**Tujuan:** SEO \+ emotional hook \+ clarity

**Elements:**

* H1: *\[City Name\] Travel Itinerary*  
* Subheadline: *Curated routes to explore \[City Name\] independently*  
* Short intro (2–3 kalimat, human & inspiring)  
* CTA utama: **Download Itinerary**  
* Secondary CTA: **View Pricing**

**SEO Notes:**

* City name wajib di H1  
* Jangan pakai jargon marketing berlebihan

---

## **2\. City Snapshot**

**Tujuan:** Quick scan untuk traveler

**Content blocks:**

* Best time to visit  
* Ideal trip length (days)  
* Travel style (culture / nature / food / urban)  
* Budget level (low–mid–comfort)  
* Who this city is for

**SEO Notes:**

* Bullet points ramah featured snippet

---

## **3\. About the City**

**Tujuan:** Context & trust

**Content:**

* 2–3 paragraf deskriptif  
* Cultural highlights  
* What makes this city unique in Indonesia

**SEO Notes:**

* Gunakan variasi keyword natural  
  (e.g. things to do in \[city\], travel guide \[city\])

---

## **4\. Top Experiences & Attractions**

**Tujuan:** Search intent utama

**Format:**

* Grid / list  
* Setiap item:  
  * Short description  
  * Why it matters

**SEO Notes:**

* Subheading H2/H3 per cluster  
* Cocok untuk long-tail keyword

---

## **5\. Sample Itinerary Preview (Free)**

**Tujuan:** Trust & teaser

**Content:**

* 1–2 day itinerary preview  
* Time-based flow (morning–afternoon–evening)  
* Tanpa detail lengkap

**CTA:**

*Unlock full itinerary for this city*

---

## **6\. Paid Itinerary Plans**

**Tujuan:** Conversion

**Content:**

* Pricing table (Essential / Complete / Explorer)  
* Highlight **Most Popular**  
* CTA: **Download Full Itinerary**

**SEO Notes:**

* Internal link ke pricing page

---

## **7\. What’s Inside the Itinerary**

**Tujuan:** Value clarity

**Checklist:**

* Daily routes  
* Transport logic  
* Local tips  
* Food & area recommendations  
* Offline access

---

## **8\. Practical Travel Information**

**Tujuan:** Trust \+ SEO utility

**Content:**

* Getting around  
* Local etiquette  
* Safety tips  
* Internet & SIM info

**SEO Notes:**

* Sangat SEO-friendly

---

## **9\. Optional Booking Links (Affiliate)**

**Tujuan:** Monetization tanpa mengganggu

**Content:**

* Flights  
* Accommodation areas  
* Local transport

**Disclaimer:**

ITINARA does not provide booking services. Links are for convenience only.

---

## **10\. FAQs (SEO Booster)**

**Tujuan:** Answer intent & ranking

**Examples:**

* Is \[City\] worth visiting?  
* How many days in \[City\]?  
* Is \[City\] safe for tourists?  
* Do I need a guide?

---

## **11\. Related Cities & Bundles**

**Tujuan:** Internal linking & upsell

**Content:**

* Nearby cities  
* Bundle offers

---

## **12\. Final CTA**

**Tujuan:** Close the loop

**CTA Copy:**

*Travel smarter. Download your \[City Name\] itinerary today.*

---

## **Technical SEO Checklist**

* Meta title: *\[City Name\] Travel Itinerary | ITINARA*  
* Meta description ≤ 155 characters  
* Slug: /itinerary/\[city-name\]  
* Optimized images (alt text city-based)  
* Page speed \< 3s  
* Schema FAQ (recommended)
